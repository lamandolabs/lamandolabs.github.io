Privacy Policy

RandomKit by Lamando Labs

Effective Date: November 2026

RandomKit ("we", "us", or "our") operates the RandomKit mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

Core Functionality: RandomKit is an offline-first application. We do not collect, store, or upload any of your personal data, lists, entries, or draw results to any server. All your data is stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services (Advertising): We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use certain information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and usage data, to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

2. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, we do not have access to it. You can delete all your data at any time by clearing the app data in your Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

3. Permissions

RandomKit requires internet access (for loading ads) and may require vibration permissions (for haptic feedback). We do not request access to your contacts, camera, location, or files.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at hello@lamandolabs.com.
