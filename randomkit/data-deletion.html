Data Deletion Request

App Name: RandomKit

Developer: Lamando Labs

1. How RandomKit Handles Data

RandomKit is a fully offline application. We do not store your lists, names, or draw histories on any server. All data is stored locally on your device.

2. How to Delete Your Data

Because we have no access to your data, you have full control to delete it instantly using one of these methods:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the app and delete individual lists or session histories manually.
  • Clear App Data: Go to your Android Settings → Apps → RandomKit → Storage → Tap "Clear Data".
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling RandomKit from your device permanently deletes all associated local data.

3. Third-Party Data (AdMob)

RandomKit uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID. To delete or reset this data, go to your Android Settings → Google → Ads → Tap "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".

4. Data Retention

Local Data: Deleted immediately when you clear app data or uninstall the app (retention period: 0 days).

AdMob Data: Retained by Google according to their policies. You can manage this via your Google account settings.

If you have any questions, contact us at hello@lamandolabs.com.
